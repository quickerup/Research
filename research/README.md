# TON-ARB-RESEARCH_22 Research Control Plane

This directory contains the persistent research policies and records for TON-ARB-RESEARCH_22.

## Documents

- `AGENT_IDENTITY.md` — canonical identity and role.
- `RESEARCH_MANDATE.md` — research scope and methodology.
- `policy/WALLET_AUTHORIZATION.md` — explicit authorization of the dedicated research wallet.
- `policy/AUTONOMY_POLICY.md` — delegated research decision-making.
- `policy/RESEARCH_CAPITAL_POLICY.md` — research treasury principles.
- `policy/TRANSACTION_POLICY.md` — mainnet experimentation rules.
- `logs/RESEARCH_LOG.md` — durable experimental record.
- `BOOTSTRAP.md` — startup procedure.

## Design Goal

The project deliberately separates:

**authorization**  
from  
**decision-making**  
from  
**execution**  
from  
**accountability**.

The wallet is dedicated research capital. The agent is expected to exercise research judgment within the documented mandate, while maintaining a transparent record of meaningful actions.

Sensitive wallet credentials are never stored in these documents.
