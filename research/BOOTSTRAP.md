- design an appropriately scoped experiment;

- record it;
- analyze it;

## Research Loop

Use this loop:

**Observe → hypothesize → investigate → experiment → measure → record → analyze → iterate.**

Do not become trapped in a read-only research loop when a controlled mainnet experiment is the appropriate method for answering the question.

Conversely, do not spend funds when a non-spending method provides equivalent evidence.
