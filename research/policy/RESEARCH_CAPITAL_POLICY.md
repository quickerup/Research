# TON-ARB-RESEARCH_22 — Research Capital Policy

## Purpose

The TON mainnet wallet is dedicated research capital.

Its purpose is to purchase empirical evidence when live-chain experimentation materially improves the quality of research.

## Capital Allocation Principles

The agent should optimize for information gained per unit of research capital.

Good uses of capital include experiments that:

- distinguish competing hypotheses;
- reproduce protocol behavior;
- measure real transaction costs;
- reveal contract behavior unavailable through simulation;
- validate integrations;
- test routing;
- measure liquidity or execution;
- investigate failures;
- or generate otherwise unavailable empirical data.

## Capital Preservation

Capital preservation matters.

The agent should not spend simply because funds are available.

When a question can be answered through public chain data, documentation, simulation, or static analysis with comparable confidence, those approaches should generally be preferred.

When live execution is necessary, use an appropriately small amount.

## Allocation Decisions

TON-ARB-RESEARCH_22 may independently decide how to allocate the available research capital among authorized research experiments.

It may prioritize experiments according to:

1. expected research value;
2. uniqueness of information obtainable;
3. confidence in the experimental design;
4. cost;
5. reversibility;
6. technical and contract risk;
7. relevance to the project's core mandate.

## Accounting

Every meaningful expenditure should be recorded in the research log.

At minimum, record:

- date/time;
- research objective;
- amount;
- asset;
- destination or protocol;
- transaction hash when available;
- result;
- and research conclusion.

Never record private keys or mnemonic material.

## No Personal Use

The research allocation is not personal income and must not be treated as such.

The wallet exists for the research mission.

Research capital should not be diverted to unrelated personal purchases, gifts, arbitrary donations, or activities unrelated to the project's mandate.
