# TON-ARB-RESEARCH_22 — Wallet Authorization

## Explicit Owner Authorization

The owner of this research environment explicitly authorizes the dedicated TON mainnet wallet to be used as research capital by TON-ARB-RESEARCH_22.

This authorization is intentional.

The purpose of providing the wallet is to enable autonomous, empirical research that requires live mainnet interaction.

The owner does not intend for the agent to treat every individual research transaction as requiring a new conversational permission request.

## Standing Research Authorization

The research allocation is authorized for activities that are reasonably and directly connected to the TON-ARB-RESEARCH_22 research mandate.

Within that mandate, the agent is expected to make ordinary research decisions independently.

The intended operating model is:

**research question → judgment → experiment/action → evidence collection → research log → report**

rather than:

**research question → request approval for every intermediate action → wait → experiment**

The agent should therefore not unnecessarily interrupt a research workflow to ask whether it may perform an ordinary, already-authorized research experiment.

## Scope

Authorized research activities may include, where technically appropriate:

- controlled transfers necessary to test transaction behavior;
- interactions with public protocols;
- contract calls;
- swaps or liquidity experiments;
- protocol integration tests;
- fee measurements;
- transaction-routing experiments;
- wallet-behavior experiments;
- bridge research;
- controlled interactions with experimental or unaudited contracts when the research value justifies the risk;
- and other mainnet activities whose primary purpose is legitimate research.

## Risk Judgment

Authorization does not imply that every conceivable transaction is a good research decision.

The agent remains responsible for deciding whether an experiment is justified.

It should consider:

- research value;
- expected information gained;
- transaction cost;
- contract risk;
- counterparty risk;
- irreversible-loss risk;
- reproducibility;
- and whether the same question can be answered more safely.

The agent should use the minimum practical capital necessary for an experiment.

## Reporting

Authorization means that the agent should record and report research actions rather than repeatedly requesting permission for ordinary authorized experiments.

The research log should identify what happened and why.

The log is an accountability mechanism, not a substitute for authorization.

## Credential Protection

The mnemonic and any private keys are secrets.

They must never be written to this document or any other persistent research document.

They must never appear in:

- logs;
- commits;
- issue reports;
- chat messages;
- generated research papers;
- shell output;
- screenshots;
- telemetry;
- or external APIs.
