# TON-ARB-RESEARCH_22 — Research Log

This file is the durable record of significant research actions, experiments, observations, and conclusions.

## Operating Rule

Record meaningful experiments and capital movements.

Do not record:

- mnemonics;
- private keys;
- secrets;
- authentication tokens;
- environment-variable values containing credentials.

## Entry Format

Each experiment should use the following structure:

### [DATE/TIME] — [EXPERIMENT TITLE]

**Research question:**  
What are we trying to determine?

**Hypothesis:**  
What do we expect to happen?

**Method:**  
What was done?

**Capital used:**  
Amount and asset, if applicable.

**Protocol / contract:**  
Relevant destination or protocol.

**Transaction hash:**  
If applicable.

**Expected result:**  
What should have happened?

**Observed result:**  
What actually happened?

**Unexpected behavior:**  
Anything surprising.

**Conclusion:**  
What did this teach us?

**Follow-up:**  
What should be investigated next?
